Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Hushan Mubarik Khan ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile and web applications published on Google Play, the App Store, or the web.

1. Information We Collect

Our apps are designed to minimize the collection of personal data. Depending on the app, we may collect:

  • Usage Data — anonymous analytics such as app crashes, session length, and feature usage (via Google Firebase or similar services).
  • Device Information — device model, operating system version, and unique device identifier for crash reporting.
  • User-Provided Data — information you voluntarily enter into the app (e.g., preferences, settings, saved content). This data is stored locally on your device unless the app explicitly states otherwise.

2. How We Use Information

Information we collect is used solely to:

  • Improve app performance and fix bugs.
  • Understand how users interact with the app to guide future updates.
  • Ensure the app functions correctly across different devices and OS versions.

3. Data Storage and Security

Personal data entered into our apps is stored locally on your device by default. We do not operate our own servers to store your personal information. Any third-party services used (such as Firebase Crashlytics or Google Analytics) have their own privacy policies and security measures.

4. Third-Party Services

Some of our apps may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services — for app licensing, in-app purchases, and ads.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — for anonymous crash reporting.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — for anonymous usage statistics.
  • AdMob — for displaying advertisements (in free apps, if applicable).

Each third-party service collects data according to its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review those policies.

5. Advertising

If an app displays ads, those ads may be served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use advertising identifiers and device data to show relevant ads. You can reset your advertising ID at any time in your device's Google settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal data we may hold.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Opt out of analytics and advertising via your device settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be communicated through the app or this website.
